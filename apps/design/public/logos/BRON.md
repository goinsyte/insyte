# Logos of the integrations

Shown wherever the console names a service it connects to, so an operator recognises the software
they already use at a glance — which a monogram never is. Each mark is the property of its owner and
is used here only to name the product Insyte connects to (nominative use). Insyte is not affiliated
with or endorsed by any of them.
